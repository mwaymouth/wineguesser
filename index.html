    How it works

    1. Sign up with your name and phone number.
    2. Taste glasses 1 through 6. For each, pick the grape and the region.
    3. Seal your sheet. Right grape: 3 points. Region: 3 for the exact spot, 2 for the right area (Bordeaux for a Saint-Émilion), 1 for just the country.
    4. At the reveal, the top score wins the bottle.
